# Room audio

The single Sound control starts and stops music and rain together. Audio is opt-in, with no preload before interaction. The files loop and remain available across portfolio routes. Motion has its own control. Hidden tabs pause audio and resume it only when sound remains enabled.

## Music

- Track: **Night Owl**, Broke For Free (2011). A mellow downtempo selection for the room; not represented as a recording from the 1990s.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Broke_For_Free_-_01_-_Night_Owl.ogg
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Conversion: full track, 44.1 kHz, 128 kbps MP3; loudness normalized to -18 LUFS, peak -2 dBTP, LRA 9.
- Playback gain: 0.38, with a 0.8-second fade-in.
